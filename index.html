✦ Our Story

Rooted in Heritage

Zyaka was born in a small Delhi kitchen in 2009, where Nana Ji's dum biryani recipe had been a family secret for three generations. The word Zyaka — meaning taste in Urdu — is our promise to you.

We source whole spices from the same Old Delhi bazaars our founders visited decades ago. Every dish is cooked in small batches, sealed with intention, and served with pride.
